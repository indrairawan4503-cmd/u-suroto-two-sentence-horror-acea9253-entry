# Version log — Variant C (dentist / body detail)

Concept lane: BODY/MEDICAL — a routine dental cleaning reveals something
that has been done to the narrator's body without their knowledge.
Fresh turn: NOT "the doctor was the killer", NOT "the stranger has my face".

## v1 — count discrepancy only
"The hygienist counted my teeth, paused, and counted again. She wrote
thirty-three on my chart, but I have had thirty-two since I was twelve."
~150 chars. FAILS: turn is just a counting error — no dread, no re-read
payoff (criterion 2), far under the byte budget. The reader shrugs.

## v2 — bus corpse (abandoned lane)
"The woman beside me on the night bus falls asleep against my shoulder
every Thursday, and I always wake her at her stop. Tonight she didn't
wake when I shook her, and her head stayed tilted exactly where I had
left it."
~230 chars. The turn ("where I had left it") implies the narrator
positioned a corpse — but it explains too much (the narrator is the
killer) and the wording is ambiguous. Dropped: weaker implication than
the dentist lane, and it hands the reader the answer (criterion 3).

## v3 — neighbour's roses (backup)
"Mrs. Alvarez waters her roses at six every evening, humming the same
three notes through the fence, and she has done it for all nine years
I've lived next door. Tonight the garden was empty, the soil was freshly
turned, and the humming was coming from underneath it."
~270 chars. Good re-read turn, but "buried under the garden" is a
familiar trope family — fails criterion 5 (originality) against a judge
who has read a thousand shocks. Kept as backup only.

## v4 — dentist, the stranger's filling
"At my six-month cleaning, the hygienist counted my teeth twice, frowned,
and wrote thirty-three on my chart. I have had thirty-two teeth since I
was twelve, and the extra one at the back already has a filling in it,
placed by a dentist I have never met."
~280 chars. Strong: one concrete scene (six-month cleaning, 33 vs 32),
turn makes sentence 1 worse (the count was the discovery of a violation),
horror implied (who placed it? when? while I slept?), fresh twist.
Needs: fuller byte budget + a closing sting.

## v5 — aged filling (shipped in main.py first run)
"At my six-month cleaning, the hygienist counted my teeth twice, frowned,
and wrote thirty-three on my chart. I have had thirty-two teeth since I
was twelve, and the extra one at the back already has a filling in it —
old, worn smooth, and placed by a dentist I have never met."
"Old, worn smooth" deepens the dread: the violation is not new, it has
been there for years. Still slightly under budget; final clause can carry
one more sting.

## v6 — FINAL (submitted text)
Adds the closing clause "in a mouth I thought was only mine": the turn
now lands on the body itself not being solely the narrator's. Re-read:
the routine cleaning, the careful counting, the filling — someone else
has been inside my mouth, for years, and I never knew.
